1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Catalystai Technology Private Limited, operating as Catalyst AI ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you visit this website or submit an enquiry through it.

Catalyst AI is an independent admissions and counselling practice for Dubai campuses. This website is not the official website of any university. Admission decisions, fees, programme content and degree awards are the sole responsibility of the respective institutions.

By using this website or submitting your details, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or submit your information.

This policy is prepared in line with the Information Technology Act 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, as applicable.

3Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide to us

When you fill in the enquiry form or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we collect:

3.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address, browser type, device type and operating system

  • Campaign parameters such as UTM source, medium and keyword

4How we use your information

We use your information to:

  1. Respond to your enquiry and provide free admissions counselling.
  2. Contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp and email regarding your enquiry and application process.
  3. Share your enquiry with the relevant Dubai campus so you can be considered for admission.
  4. Help you connect with education loan providers, only where you request loan assistance.
  5. Maintain records and improve our counselling quality.
  6. Analyse website traffic and measure the performance of our advertising.
  7. Show relevant advertisements on Google and Meta platforms.
  8. Detect and prevent fraud, spam and security incidents.
  9. Comply with applicable laws and lawful requests from authorities.

We process your personal information on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the form and tick the consent box. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us at the address in Section 14. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

By submitting your mobile number, you authorise us to contact you by call, SMS and WhatsApp, even if your number is on the DND / NCPR register, for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. You can ask us to stop at any time by telling the caller, replying STOP, or emailing us.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Education loan partners, only if you request loan assistance.
  • Service providers (CRM, telephony, email, cloud hosting, analytics and advertising platforms) who act on our behalf.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and legal authorities, where required by law.
  • A successor business, if we merge or transfer our business, subject to this policy's protections.

International transfers

Campus partners may operate outside India, so your information may be shared with or accessed from other countries. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with this policy and applicable law.

We use them to understand how visitors use the website, measure campaign performance, and show relevant advertisements to previous visitors (remarketing).

  • Block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • Google ads personalisation: adssettings.google.com
