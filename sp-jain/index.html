How admission works

Submit your application together with an aptitude test score.

01

Application screening.

If you are shortlisted, the admissions committee invites you to the next stage.

02

Interviews and evaluation.

A business creativity test, an essay and case discussion, and a personal interview. You may attend the interview online or on campus.

03

Decision.

You receive an offer, a waitlist place or a decline. SP Jain's process states that you hear within about five days.

04

SP Jain states that decisions are communicated within approximately five days of your interview. We help you prepare your application and walk you through each stage — book a free call to get started.
